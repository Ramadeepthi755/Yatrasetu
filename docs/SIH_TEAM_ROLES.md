## 2. Adaptation for Smaller / Larger Teams
- **3-Member Team**:
  - Member 1: Problem + Solution + Traveler Demo & AI.
  - Member 2: Partner Ecosystem + Government Intelligence & Action Center Demo.
  - Member 3: Technical Architecture + Security + Q&A.
- **4-Member Team**:
  - Member 1: Intro + Traveler Experience.
  - Member 2: Partner Portal + Travel Connect.
  - Member 3: Government Intelligence + Policy Action.
  - Member 4: Technical Stack + Architecture + Q&A.
- **6-Member Team**:
  - Assign an additional member as **Live Operator / Screen Driver** ensuring flawless browser navigation while speakers present.

---

## 3. Communication Guidelines During Judge Q&A
1. **Never Contradict a Teammate**: Support and expand on technical points smoothly.
2. **Be Data-Honest**: If asked about physical sensors or real banking charges, reiterate our transparent boundaries (e.g., *"We measure digital demand proxies rather than sensor counts, and payments are in demonstration simulation mode"*).
3. **Be Concise**: Direct, punchy answers that respect the judge's time.
