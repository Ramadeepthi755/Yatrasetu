# 📊 YatraSetu — SIH Presentation Slide Structure

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Slide Title**: YatraSetu — Discover India. Connect Locally. Grow Tourism.
- **Visual**: High-resolution branding banner with triangular ecosystem icon (Traveler $\leftrightarrow$ Partner $\leftrightarrow$ Government).
- **Key Bullets**:

### Slide 2: The National Tourism Challenge
- **Slide Title**: Structural Imbalance in Indian Tourism
- **Visual**: Infographic showing 70% footfall concentrated in 15 hotspots vs. overlooked regional gems.
- **Key Bullets**:
  - **Severe Overtourism**: Infrastructure and ecological strain at major hubs.
  - **Discovery Fragmentation**: Hundreds of culturally rich Tier-B/C destinations overlooked.
  - **Economic Exclusion**: Local guides and homestays excluded by high-commission OTAs (15–30%).
  - **Government Decision Lag**: Lack of real-time carrying-capacity intelligence.
- **Speaker Dialogue**: *"India has incredible heritage, but 70% of tourists crowd into just 15 hubs, causing overtourism while local homestays struggle and tourism boards lack actionable demand data."*
- **Time**: 35 seconds.

---

### Slide 3: The YatraSetu Solution
- **Slide Title**: The 3-Sided Connected Ecosystem
- **Visual**: Triangular architecture diagram linking Traveler, Local Partner, and Government.
- **Key Bullets**:
  - **Traveler**: Verified exploration, Zero-Hallucination AI planner, Travel Connect.
  - **Local Partner**: Direct listing portal, verified guide badging, zero middlemen commission.
  - **Government**: National Intelligence Hub, carrying capacity proxies, demand redistribution.
- **Speaker Dialogue**: *"YatraSetu solves this by bridging the entire ecosystem. Traveler activity directly generates demand intelligence for government governance, which in turn redistributes tourism to empower local communities."*
- **Time**: 35 seconds.
