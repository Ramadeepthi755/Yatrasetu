## 1. Executive Summary for Tourism Authorities
State Tourism Boards, District Tourism Promotion Councils (DTPCs), and the Ministry of Tourism face a common challenge: **decision lag**. Traditional tourism statistics rely on annual post-facto surveys and ticket counters that report what happened months ago rather than what is happening now.

YatraSetu provides a **real-time, leading-indicator National Tourism Intelligence Hub** enabling data-driven carrying capacity governance, hidden-gem discovery, demand redistribution, and targeted ecosystem investment.

## 3. Clear Terminology & Provenance Distinction

To maintain absolute institutional credibility during SIH evaluation and government deployment, YatraSetu strictly distinguishes platform indicators from official census data:

| Metric | What YatraSetu Measures | Why It Matters for Government | NOT Claimed As |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Activity Pressure** | Relative volume of traveler interest, trip planning, and booking inquiries on the platform. | Acts as a **leading indicator** of impending physical arrival, giving authorities time to prepare traffic, parking, and crowd management. | Physical IoT turnstile counts or live satellite censuses. |
| **Destination Health** | Normalized 0–100 composite index balancing demand, carrying capacity, host density, transit, and sustainability. | Provides a single holistic benchmark to prioritize state tourism funding and infrastructure grants. | Official State Pollution Control Board environmental compliance certifications. |
| **Transparent Baseline Forecast** | Mathematical exponential moving average weighted by seasonal window peaks. | Provides a reproducible planning baseline for seasonal staffing and logistics. | An AI-guaranteed arrival prophecy. |
| **Ecosystem Gaps** | Algorithmic ratio comparing registered attractions against registered local guides and homestays. | Identifies where state skill development programs (e.g., guide training) will have the highest immediate impact. | Official Ministry of Labour employment censuses. |

### Scenario A: Alleviating Overtourism at Hyper-Crowded Hubs
- **Observation**: Goa reaches an Activity Pressure of 88 (Classification: `HIGH_PRESSURE`).
- **Engine Output**: The Redistribution Engine pairs Goa with *Gokarna* and *Sindhudurg* ($\le 150\text{ km}$, Relief Potential: High).

### Scenario B: Addressing Regional Guide Bottlenecks
- **Observation**: *Nalanda / Rajgir* records strong traveler itinerary interest, but has 0 verified registered local guides in the ecosystem.
- **Engine Output**: Ecosystem Gap Detector raises an amber alert: `GUIDE_SHORTAGE (Urgency: High)`.
- **Government Action**: State tourism board initiates a local youth certification workshop for heritage interpretation.
